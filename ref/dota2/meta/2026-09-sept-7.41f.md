# meta snapshot, September 2026, patch 7.41f

Collected 2026-09-24 from tier list coverage. Cross-source consensus only, single source picks noted as such.

## consensus picks

- Pos 1 / carry: Spectre and Lifestealer are the most reliable pos 1 picks (playhub), both S tier (sportskeeda 7.41e list, playerauctions). Sportskeeda S tier: Spectre, Terrorblade, Lifestealer, Slark, Shadow Fiend, Ursa. A tier includes Anti-Mage, Faceless Void. Wraith King and Lifestealer called out as dominating via innates (playerauctions, BSJ: WK skeletons at level 3, Luna glaives level 4-5 with treads).
- Mid: Keeper of the Light and Snapfire lead the mid pool (playhub, single source, treat as soft). Storm Spirit in the overall best list (skycoach).
- Offlane: Axe top tier across sources (skycoach best overall, 7.41d nerf confirms he was a problem).
- Support: Ogre Magi and Jakiro in the overall best list (skycoach). Ogre was also nerf-adjacent attention in 7.41d coverage.

## patch trajectory

- 7.41 (March): Lycan and Lifestealer S tier early in the patch (immortalboost). Phoenix nerfed during the series.
- 7.41d (June): Tiny, Kez, Invoker, Ember, Hoodwink, Axe nerfed, so tier lists after June should show those falling off slightly.
- 7.41f (Sept 15): small touch-ups only (KotL, SF, Slardar, items).
