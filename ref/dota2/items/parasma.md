# Parasma

Verified 2026-09-24 against Dota 2 Wiki (Fandom). Introduced patch 7.35 (December 2023), still current in 7.41 series.

## stats and cost

- Total cost: 5975 gold, including a 400 gold recipe.
- Components: Witch Blade (approx 2775, base shop) + Mystic Staff (approx 2800, secret shop) + recipe 400.
- +40 intelligence, +40 attack speed, +7 armor, +1.5 mana regeneration, +300 projectile speed.
- Derived from the 40 int: +480 mana, +2 mana regeneration, +4% base magic resistance.
- Classified as a support item in the shop, base shop components plus secret shop staff.

## read

An int-scaling right click item built around Witch Blade's attack modifier. Users are int carries and aggressive utility cores (Windranger-likes, Arc Warden uses it in 7.41 builds). The +300 projectile speed is a real dps multiplier for slow-projectile heroes, not a vanity stat.
