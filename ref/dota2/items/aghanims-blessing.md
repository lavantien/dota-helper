# Aghanim's Blessing

Verified 2026-09-24 against Dota 2 Wiki (Fandom) and 7.41 patch coverage.

## what it is

- A consumable purchasable at the base shop under the magical category.
- Upgrades the owner's Aghanim's Scepter so the scepter upgrade no longer requires an item slot: the blessing is instantly consumed on acquisition, the scepter passive becomes innate.
- The shard version of this concept existed before (Aghanim's Blessing for shards), the 7.41 era item covers the scepter.

## patch notes touching it

- 7.41: Aghanim's Scepter reworks for several heroes (Diviner's Deck gained a new scepter passive, at least one hero lost a scepter upgrade entirely).
- 7.41d bug fix: Invoker was not receiving an Aghanim's Blessing from Alchemist casting Aghanim's Scepter on him.

## read

Late-game slot efficiency for any scepter-reliant hero: after blessing, the scepter slot frees up for a 6th combat item. On scaling cores (Lifestealer's aghs-blessing build, Arc Warden doubling items) this is effectively a whole extra item slot bought for gold.
