# Largo (new hero, December 2025)

Verified 2026-09-24 against dota2.com hero page and coverage.

## identity

- Released December 16, 2025. The "frog bard" hero previously leaked as Bard Frog.
- Melee, roles: support, disabler, durable.
- Theme: grants his team damage, healing, or speed, and dispels friends or foes "using just the right lick".

## kit

- Q: Catchy Lick
- W: Frogstomp
- E: Croak of Genius
- R: Amphibian Rhapsody
- Known talents: +15 Frogstomp damage, +1% Croak of Genius max health dps.
- Signature mechanic: Rhapsody, adaptive damage/healing/speed provision plus dispels.

## read

A flexible fight support with dispels and adaptive buffs, not a unit spammer. He slots into the "scalable teamfight support" bucket and is currently a real answer to Spectre. Expect him in low MMR games since he is new enough to be popular.
