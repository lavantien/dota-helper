# eval harness

Order-aware evaluation of the picker against real drafts. `make eval` (engine `eval report`) rebuilds the picker model from the live duckdb at emitted precision, replays every committed league draft in pick order, and writes `latest.json` beside this readme plus a summary to stdout. The report is deterministic: no clock stamps, map keys sort, identical db and config give identical bytes.

## model parity

The replay scores the exact values `picker-data-generated.js` ships. `eval.BuildModel` loads through `emit.LoadPicker`, then rerounds every value through `emit.RoundFixed` (the float readback of the same `strconv.FormatFloat` string the artifact prints): mu and syn cells, popularity, and the prior midrank percentile derived over pool-ordered prior values exactly as `FormatPicker` does. Gates are evaluated in slug space with the inert `roleCandidatesGated` baseline (`CandidatesGated = MinInt`, the go twin of picker-score.js's `-Infinity`), so a gated-count condition can never recurse through its own count.

Parity rule, restated: any structural change to scoring must update `engine/internal/analytics/score.go`, `picker/picker-score.js`, `picker/DATA-CONTRACT.md`, and the emit goldens in the same commit. The accumulation order in `Score` is load-bearing for bit-level agreement.

## replay semantics

`SequentialReplay` walks each draft by `seq`. At a pool hero's pick the information state is: allies are the pool heroes picked earlier on the picking side (non-pool allies are invisible, a documented approximation, because the picker's syn rows only exist within the pool), visible enemies are every earlier opposing pick, and `taken` covers all earlier picks and bans. Bans never enter the ally or enemy sets, they only remove heroes from the candidate field, and a ban's side is irrelevant to that. Candidates are pool heroes not taken and not hard-gated; a candidate is eligible when at least one of its config roles survives the gate evaluation, and per-role standings are computed for each role the picked hero plays. A picked hero that was itself hard-gated still gets an at-pick score (it feeds the advantage sums) but no percentile.

`FinalSetReplay` is the ablation twin: allies and enemies are the final sets, leave-one-pick-out over the pool picks, everything else identical. The gap between the two replays measures how much the picker's rankings depend on order information versus final composition.

`DraftAdvantage` is the radiant sum of at-pick scores minus the dire sum. The AUC set keeps only matches where both sides drafted at least `eval.minSidePool` pool heroes so the advantage signal is two-sided.

## metrics

- mean pick percentile: `(n - rank) / (n - 1)` over the full-pool ranking, 1 is the top pick; picks against single-candidate fields and gated-out picks are skipped.
- any-role top-k hit rate: the share of picks whose hero placed in the top k of at least one of its role rankings (`eval.topK`). Small role fields cap the ceiling: a 3-hero role always hits top 3.
- calibration: score-decile bins with edges fitted on train pick scores (nearest-rank quantiles, upper-edge inclusive so a score exactly on an edge lands in the bin below it, tied edges collapse), realized win rate of the picking side per bin on the holdout, plus a count of adjacent nonempty bins whose rate drops.
- draft-level AUC: the ch18 concordance of `DraftAdvantage` against the radiant win label (`stats.AUC`).
- bootstrap intervals: the ch17 percentile bootstrap over match resamples (`stats.PercentileCI`, config `eval.bootstrap`, 2.5/97.5), picks grouped by match so a resample redraws matches, not picks.

One known interval edge: a bootstrap resample that draws no ranked picks contributes a 0 to the percentile bootstrap distribution, which only bites when ranked picks are sparse relative to matches (the current run ranks every pick).

Split: matches ordered by `(start_time, match_id)`, the leading `eval.split.trainFrac` fraction is train (boundary is the truncated product, pinned by test), the rest is holdout. All headline metrics are holdout; train only feeds the calibration edges.

## aggregate audit

`Audit` tests the raw pair tables the model was mined from, per family (matchup enemies, synergy allies). For each hero, cells with `win_count` and `n >= eval.screen.minMatches` are tested against the hero's remaining base in the same family: a 2x2 chi-square of the cell `(w, n)` against `(W-w, N-n)` (ch29), falling back to the exact two-sided binomial point-probability test (ch39) when any expected count runs under the ch29 floor of 5. Holm and BH run per family at `eval.screen.q` (ch40), survivor counts land in the report, and a Spearman rank correlation compares the normalized pct against the raw lift `(w/n) / (W/N)` over cells that carry a normalized value. Rows where wins exceed matches drop entirely; single-cell heroes have no complement and are not tested. Perspective: `stratz_vs` rows serve the pool hero's own wins and the hero-positive `synergy` sign, verified in-db two ways (within-row `sign(synergy)` agrees with `sign(winCount/matches - 0.5)` on 94.6% of rows with `|synergy| >= 3pp`, and the pooled per-hero rate sits 1.35pp from the independent opendota snapshot versus 3.61pp complemented), so counts pass through unchanged; explorer-sourced counts are hero-perspective too.

## book-technique diagnostics

Three report sections place the picker beside the KDD techniques the engine already ships; all three are diagnostics only, nothing here feeds the live score. The `enabled` flags on `eval.triples`, `eval.seq`, and `eval.ensemble` stay reserved for future live integration and do not gate the sections.

**Comparators** (`comparators`): the ch19 multivariate-Bernoulli naive Bayes and the ch6/ch20 cosine kNN over one-hot final-set drafts train on the train split and score every holdout match, beside a `picker` row that replays the same sequential draft advantage the ensemble consumes. Each row carries its draft-level AUC and a bootstrap interval over holdout match resamples. The ch23 bagged blend follows: `eval.ensemble.bags` bootstrap resamples of the train split each fit both comparators, the bag averages blend with the picker advantage under ascent-fitted weights `[picker, naiveBayes, knn]` (grid from `eval.fit`, seed from `eval.bootstrap.seed`), and the blend gets its own holdout AUC and interval.

**Triples** (`triples`): the ch23-28 vertical apriori over pool-restricted side events, mined at `eval.triples.minSupport` for the embedded singles/pairs/triples with lift and conviction. The `sweep` re-reads one floor mining (support 1) and reports cumulative pair and triple counts at every distinct pair support the data shows, so the configured threshold's place in the observed support landscape is data-derived.

**Pick order** (`seq`): the ch30 directional census over the train drafts' pick streams: per-side opening picks, the pooled response transitions (`P(our next = to | enemy just picked from)`), and the direction-asymmetry census under `eval.seq.censusMinCount` and `eval.seq.censusRatio` (a pair counts as compared when its busier direction reaches the count floor; one-directional pairs are asymmetric by definition, two-sided pairs when the busier direction carries at least the ratio times the quieter one).

**Mine screen A/B, measured.** The ch29/40 significance screen (`eval.screen.enabled`, the alpha-raising verdicts inside `mine.EffectiveAlphas`) was A/B measured on this corpus after the hero-perspective fix and the alpha promotions: enabled it reads holdout mean percentile 0.5997 [0.5757, 0.6243] against 0.6008 [0.5785, 0.6243] disabled, a 0.0011 cost inside noise with no interval clearing the promotion bar (top1 and top3 move the same direction; only calibration monotonicity violations improve, 5 to 3). The screen stays disabled: with the fitted matchup alpha at 1633 the screen's fail-alpha multiple adds no measurable denoising on top of the shrink. Re-measure after any future alpha promotion moves the shrink landscape.

## constant fits and promotion

Phase 4 removed hand-set scoring constants: the weights, gate deltas, shrinkage alphas, and completion rank/lambda are fitted, and `ref/dota2/eval/derivations.json` is the manifest recording how every scoring-affecting key under `weights`, `gateDeltas`, `shrink`, `completion`, `score`, and `normalize` got its value (`make check` gates coverage and value sync). Entries are `fitted` (measured by the procedures below; `pending` until a promotion stamps `measured`), `justified` (fixed by the definition they name: slot counts are rules of the game, `score.midPct` is the fair midpoint, `score.flexHalf` is the percentile-scale normalization divisor, `normalize.zSdFloor` and the completion solver numerics are guards), or `authored` (`score.flexCap`, the popularity horizon, flagged as the one future promote-to-fitted candidate).

**Objective pivot, and its evidence.** The fits optimize the mean pick percentile, not the draft-level AUC. The committed phase 3 report measured the AUC at 0.4979 [0.3960, 0.5996] on this population: chance, with an interval that cannot support any objective. The mean pick percentile is the signal-bearing metric (0.5436 [0.5161, 0.5699] over 546 holdout picks, 204 holdout matches of 677). So the weight fit maximizes the train percentile and the promote guard reads the holdout percentile; AUC and top-k ride along in the proposal file as report-only context.

**Weight fit** (`make eval-fit`, `engine eval fit weights`): the sequential replay's per-pick candidate fields are cached once as Term breakdowns plus fired bonus and penalty gate rule counts (the gate delta decomposes exactly into `bonusCount * gateDeltas.bonus + penaltyCount * gateDeltas.penalty`, pinned by test against `gates.Evaluate`). The cached score is a linear recombination of the five fitted weights, the authored per-role `weights.synByRole` map (applied through each cached row's picking role, never fitted), and the two gate deltas, bit-identical to `analytics.Score` for the live vector. Coordinate ascent sweeps each fitted coefficient over the `eval.fit` grid (-maxWeight..+maxWeight in gridStep steps, 0 included), fixed field order, up to `maxPasses`, stopping when a pass stops strictly improving; ties keep the earliest value. The artifact lands at `paths.fitOut` (`var/eval-fit.json`, gitignored) with the train and holdout percentiles, the paired bootstrap gain interval over holdout match resamples, the ablation table (each fitted coefficient zeroed on the holdout), the report-only AUC and top-k, and the baseline vector the gain was measured against, synByRole included (a hub that moved since makes the proposal stale and promote refuses it).

**Shrinkage alphas** (`make eval-fit-alphas`): per family (matchup, synergy, overall), empirical-Bayes moment matching over the pooled raw aggregate tables (the crawl-wide `matchup_raw`/`synergy_raw` counts, never the thin 677-match league corpus): the population variance of the pair deltas decomposes into true between-pair variance `tau2` plus mean sampling variance `sigma2/n` with `sigma2 = 2500 pp^2` (`mine.PPVar`), and `alpha = sigma2/tau2`. `tau2` at the floor means sampling noise explains the whole spread and the current alpha stands, reason recorded. A cross-check sweeps `eval.alphaFit.grid` by mean binomial deviance of the shrunk aggregate prediction against league-holdout pair win rates where a pair's holdout n clears `alphaFit.minHoldoutN`; under the 30-pair adequacy floor the moment estimate stands (league coverage is thin on this population) and each proposal entry records which estimate won and why. The overall family pools the same hero-perspective stratz_vs sums `mine.OverallWR` pools — that perspective was adjudicated against the independent opendota snapshot (per-hero `sum(win_count)/sum(matches)` correlates +0.81 with the snapshot wr, 1.3pp mean gap versus 3.6pp for the complement). The bootstrap CI is over pair resamples.

**Completion rank/lambda** (`make eval-fit-completion`): masked-cell cross-validation on the shrunk tables. `maskFrac` of the observed cells (seeded, deterministic) is held out per family matrix (enemy and ally, the same cell membership `NormalizeMatrix` completes), ALS refits per `(rank, lambda)` in the `eval.completionFit` grids reusing `analytics.Complete`, and the winner is the best mean `stats.Spearman` between predicted and held-out shrunk values, ties to the smaller rank then lambda. The guard's interval bootstraps the winner-minus-current Spearman difference over held-out cell resamples.

**Promotion** (`make eval-promote SECTION=weights|alphas|completion`): the proposal is re-read from `var/`, the guard re-checked against the live config (weights: `gain >= eval.fit.minHoldoutGain` and `gainCiLow > 0` against the recorded baseline vector; alphas: per family, the bootstrap CI excludes the current value or the family keeps it; completion: the difference CI low above 0), and only then is `config.json` rewritten through unmarshal plus `MarshalIndent` (round trip pinned lossless by the config tests, reload-verified) with the manifest entries stamped `measured`. The make target then rebuilds the chain: alphas and completion flow through the mined tables, weights through the emitted picker data. A failing guard leaves every file untouched and the measured proposal stands as the record of the attempt.

**Open measurement caveats.** Facts that color the promoted numbers. The stratz_vs sign question is resolved: the served columns are hero-perspective (the in-row and cross-source evidence above), so the `canonicalPP` negation and the audit complement were removed, the mined tables and emitted data were regenerated, and the weights were re-fitted afterward — numbers measured before that fix (the first weight promotion, the first alpha promotion) predate the corrected pipeline. The alphas are not sign-invariant the way an early note claimed: the matchup family pooled mixed-source cells at the time (stratz_vs and dotabuff on one pair; pooling has been tier-exclusive per pair since the 2026-09-27 strict-priority re-cut), so flipping only the stratz_vs component moved the pooled spread — the fresh post-fix moment estimate read 1565.7 against the pre-fix 1633.6, and the guard kept the promoted value only because the bootstrap CI [1076.1, 2884.0] contains it (synergy and overall are whole-family or count-based and genuinely unaffected). Alpha proposals carry no baseline-staleness guard beyond the value CI, so a stale `var/eval-fit-alphas.json` can promote as a value no-op and re-stamp old measurements as current: re-fit alphas after any upstream data or section change before promoting them.

Second boundary, 2026-09-27: everything before that date predates the scope re-cut (per-hero aggregates mode-scoped to ranked all pick via the win* family, mine pooling under strict stratz-only priority, guide matchups from the mined z instead of dotabuff dis). On the re-scoped pipeline all three fit guards kept the promoted constants: the overall-alpha moment 330.0 CI [206.6, 852.0] contains the current 500, the completion winner equals the current rank 8 lambda 0.1, and the weight proposal's holdout gain +0.0066 CI [-0.0115, 0.0236] includes zero. The committed report regenerated under the kept constants reads sequential 0.5613 with the stale-weight mismatch the guard refused to trade away.

The gate deltas: the hero-perspective refit moved `bonus` from the authored 0.25 to 0 (the rules fire on the train cache, the ascent strictly preferred crediting them nothing) and left `penalty` at its incumbent, so both ablation rows are uninformative (zeroing an already-zero bonus is the identity; an unmoved penalty zeroes to nothing) and the promoting commit's "unfired" and "every coefficient" claims were wrong on these two. Live consequence, accepted with the promotion: the 12 authored bonus rules still fire and render their notes in the picker UI while shifting the score by exactly 0. Revisit when the gate deltas re-fit after the next upstream change.

The promoted exposure and flexibility sit exactly on the `eval.fit.maxWeight` 2 grid edge. A maxWeight 4 experiment read exposure 4, flexibility 1.9, prior 0, knownMu 0.2 with holdout point gain +0.0168 but a paired CI of [-0.0036, 0.0390]: the guard refused it, so the bound stays 2 and the promoted vector stands as the one the holdout can confirm. Related: the 204-match holdout has now gated two weight promotions and the alpha promotions, so each guarded gain is conditionally optimistic on a set earlier guards already consumed; there is no rotating split in the harness. The syn term ramps `a/enemySlots` (not allySlots) by long-standing parity-consistent design in both twins and the fit cache — read the syn contribution against that ramp shape. The fitted global `knownSyn` was retired on 2026-09-27: the promoted -0.5 was ablation-inert (zeroing it moved holdout percentile +0.0014) and the next fit's +0.5 proposal failed its bootstrap CI guard, so the sign of the global coefficient was never something the holdout could pin. It is replaced by the authored `weights.synByRole` map (supports full weight, cores reduced, mid weakest), a product decision recorded as authored derivation entries, and the fit now measures the other five weights under that fixed map.

The screen A/B verdict above rests on two overlapping marginal intervals, not the paired difference the weight-fit guard uses, and the on arm ran under weights fitted with the screen off; the paired machinery is the standard a future re-measurement should meet.

## population caveat

The replay population is the stratz league captains-mode backfill (`match_raw` + `draft_timing`, unranked practice lobbies from `league.matches`), which differs from the public divine-immortal ranked all-pick aggregates the picker's mu/syn/prior values are mined from. League drafts are also captains mode, so pick order is real, but hero pools, brackets, and coordination levels all shift. The report carries a population profile (counts by game_mode, lobby_type, average_rank, source, mean duration) and freezes this caveat in `populationNote`; read the headline numbers as picker quality on this population, not as a ranked-/pub-population estimate.
