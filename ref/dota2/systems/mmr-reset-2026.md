# MMR rescale 2026 (Summer Scrub)

Verified 2026-09-24, official source dota2.com/summerscrub2026.

## facts

- All players are now between 1 and 9500 MMR. Immortal rank players had their MMR adjusted (compressed).
- Immortal Draft cutoff is 8500.
- Relative player positioning was preserved, the rescale kept order, just compressed the range.

## background

- Community context: the reset was considered overdue because the old highest rank cutoff sat around 5.5k MMR while top players exceeded 17k. (Reddit r/DotA2 thread "They finally did it. The MMR reset".)
- Since patch 7.33 there is no scheduled forced seasonal reset. Standard "recalibration" resets rank confidence to 0, making each game worth a larger MMR swing, it does not wipe MMR. (Dotabuff topics, neznakov.ru.)
- Third party trackers note medals now sit roughly 200 to 500 MMR higher than in 2025 (teamsmurf.com), i.e. post-rescale medal brackets map to different absolute numbers than the old scale.

## implications for climbing

- Post-reset calibration games swing big MMR, the first 10 to 15 ranked games after the rescale are worth far more than the following hundred.
- On the new scale, old-bracket intuition ("2k = herald/guardian behavior") still roughly holds at the low end since low MMR was barely compressed, but comparisons to pre-2026 MMR numbers are invalid.
