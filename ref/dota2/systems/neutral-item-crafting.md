# neutral item crafting system (Madstone, artifacts, enchantments)

Verified 2026-09-24 against Liquipedia and coverage. System introduced in 7.38, still live with 7.41 adjustments.

## how it works

- Fully clearing neutral creep camps yields Madstone. Half-cleared camps give nothing, full clears matter.
- Madstone is spent to craft neutral items called artifacts, organized in tiers 1 to 5 with different crafting costs per tier.
- Enchantments are upgrades or modifiers applied to crafted neutral items. Think "choose artifact base + enchantment combo per game state".
- 7.41 adjustments: tier 1 availability moved to 0:00 (a tier 1 neutral can be crafted before creeps meet), tier 1 Madstone cost raised 5 to 6, artifact choices increased from 4 to 5 starting at tier 2.
- Example tier 2 artifact: Valor, with an active castable on allies.

## practical rules

- Full-clear every camp you touch, walking away from a half-clear camp wastes Madstone.
- Craft tier 1 from minute 0, it is free value in the laning phase.
- From tier 2 onward you pick from 5 artifacts, so know which artifact and enchantment you want for your hero and game state before the choice pops.
