# patch 7.41 series (March to September 2026)

Verified against official patch pages and coverage, session date 2026-09-24.

## 7.41 (March 24-25, 2026), the big one

- Facets removed from the game entirely (system introduced in 7.36 is gone).
- Innate abilities no longer scale with other abilities' levels. All innates that used to scale that way now give fixed values or scale with hero level instead.
- 9 new items added.
- Neutral items: tier 1 availability moved from 5:00 to 0:00. Madstone crafting cost for tier 1 increased 5 to 6. Artifact choices increased 4 to 5 starting from tier 2.
- Map: Roshan and Tormentor swapped sides. Tormentor now spawns bottom side, on low ground, around 20 minutes. Twin Gate highground area smaller, reworked with 3 stairs (one to the new Tormentor area, one back to lane, one elsewhere). Twin Gates now on high ground and more isolated. Camp demotions shifted jungle routing. Lotus Pool terrain changes.
- Tormentor 7.41 verified numbers: shield base barrier 2000 to 3000, damage reflection 50% to 30%, rewards slightly reduced. Combined with the low-ground spawn it is no longer a solo objective for a farming core. Treat it as a 3+ hero post-fight objective. Aghanim's Shard purchase with gold at 15+ is the correct substitute when your spike needs it.
- Health restoration mechanics adjusted.
- Community framing: "Spring Cleaning" style simplification pass, some debate it went too far.

## 7.41d (June 4, 2026), balance letter patch

- Tiny: Insurmountable (innate) slow resist reduced to 15%, Tree Grab cooldown increased, Tree Throw cast range reduced. Characterized by BSJ as accumulated "comfort nerfs", a real overall hit.
- Kez: 2 abilities plus innate weakened.
- Other nerfs: Invoker, Ember Spirit, Hoodwink, Axe.
- Buffs to underperformers, includes KotL (max charges 2 + 1 per 7 levels changed to 2 + 1 per 6), Mirana (Starstorm second meteor damage 70% to 80%), Morphling base strength.
- Bug fix: Invoker was not receiving Aghanim's Blessing from Alchemist's scepter cast.

## 7.41e (August 2026)

Letter patch, existed per tier list coverage dated 2026-08-23 "updated for 7.41e". No details captured this session.

## 7.41f (September 15, 2026), current at session date

Hero changes (partial capture):

- Keeper of the Light: base attack speed 100 to 90, Chakra Magic cooldown reductions lowered 3/4/5/6 to 2.5/3.5/4.5/5.5, Illuminate adjustments.
- Shadow Fiend: Necromastery-style bonus damage per soul 3 to 2, level 15 talent Presence armor reduction +2 to +1.5.
- Slardar: base health regen decreased.

Item changes:

- Infused Raindrops: mana regen bonus +0.8 to +0.6.
- Battle Fury: Chop Tree cooldown 4s to 3s.
- Daedalus: recipe cost increased.

Bug fixes (full list from PatchBot):

- Fixed map spot near top Roshan pit where a hero could hide inside cave geometry.
- Fixed marking unsellable items for sale during initial sellback period.
- Fixed Tranquil Boots not going on cooldown when disassembled from stash.
- Fixed Bane's Nightmare removing vision from debuff immune targets.
- Fixed Bounty Hunter's Big Game Hunter not granting bonus gold.
- Fixed Elder Titan's Astral Spirit being able to Echo Stomp while being recalled.
- Fixed Lone Druid's Spirit Bear remaining under original player control when swapping heroes.
- Fixed Lina level 25 critical talent interactions with other crit sources.
- Fixed Spirit Breaker's Planar Pocket redirecting Alchemist's Aghanim's Scepter and Moon Shard's Consume, and interactions with Linken's Sphere.
- Fixed Timbersaw's Flamethrower revealing the hero on the minimap.
